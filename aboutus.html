How it started

A Journey from
Seoul to Your Table.

It started with a simple idea: recreate the electric atmosphere of a Korean BBQ street stall, where the sizzle of meat and the laughter of friends fill the air. Our founders traveled across South Korea, learning the secrets of traditional marinades and the art of charcoal grilling.

Today, SEOUL BBQ is a tribute to that journey. We source only the finest cuts, prepare our banchan fresh daily, and use authentic smokeless charcoal to ensure your dining experience is nothing short of extraordinary.

What We Stand For

Our philosophy is simple: premium ingredients, authentic methods, and an unforgettable experience.

Authentic Charcoal

We refuse shortcuts. Our traditional smokeless charcoal provides the perfect, even heat and that unmistakable smoky flavor.

Premium Sourcing

From perfectly marbled Hanwoo-style beef to thick-cut pork belly, we hand-select every cut to ensure the highest quality.

Shared Experience

We believe food is best enjoyed together. Our table-side grills are designed to bring people closer and spark conversations.

The Art of Banchan

In Korea, a meal is never complete without a spread of Banchan. From our signature spicy kimchi and pickled radishes to fresh spinach and seasoned bean sprouts, our side dishes are prepared daily from scratch and served bottomless. It's our way of ensuring you never leave hungry.
