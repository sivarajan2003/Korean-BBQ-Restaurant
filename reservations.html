Good to Know

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel or modify your reservation free of charge up to 24 hours before your booking time. For large groups (6+), we require 48 hours' notice.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions?

Yes. We offer gluten-free soy sauce and vegetarian options for our Banchan. Please note your requirements in the special requests box.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. We recommend avoiding loose sleeves near the grill, but we provide aprons upon request!
